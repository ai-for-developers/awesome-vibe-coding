GLM-5.2 is Z.ai’s (formerly Zhipu AI) flagship open-weight model — a 753-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts design released under the MIT license, with a 1M-token context window and full downloadable weights.

Why it stands out

  • MIT-licensed, 753B-parameter MoE weights, fully self-hostable with no usage restrictions.
  • 1M-token context window with up to 131K tokens of output in a single response.
  • Beats GPT-5.5 on FrontierSWE at roughly a sixth of the cost, per Zhipu’s own benchmarking.
  • Selectable High/Max reasoning modes, letting you trade latency for depth on harder multi-step coding tasks.

Good for

Teams that want a genuinely open, frontier-tier coding model to self-host, fine-tune, or run at scale without per-token lock-in.
