Internal Comms packages format- and tone-specific guidelines for recurring internal writing tasks — progress/plans/problems updates, company newsletters, FAQ answers, leadership updates, and incident reports — loading the matching template based on which type of communication is requested.

## Why a skill for this

Each internal comms format has its own expected structure and tone that's tedious to re-specify every time — a status update reads nothing like an incident report. Packaging the format rules once means consistent output without restating house style in every prompt.

## Good for

Recurring internal writing — weekly status updates, project updates, newsletters, and incident postmortems — that needs to match an organization's established format.
