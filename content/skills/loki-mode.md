Loki Mode orchestrates dozens of coordinated AI agents to build, deploy, and operate a complete startup end-to-end, from a PRD through to a live, revenue-generating product.

## Why a skill for this

Coordinating many agents toward one large goal needs explicit role division and hand-off structure, or the agents duplicate work or conflict. This skill packages that orchestration pattern rather than leaving it to be improvised per project.

## Good for

Ambitious, largely autonomous build-and-operate projects that intentionally span far beyond a single coding session.
