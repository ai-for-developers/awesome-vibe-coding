Doc Co-Authoring packages a structured process for writing technical specs, PRDs, decision docs, and proposals: gathering context up front, iteratively building sections through brainstorming and editing, then stress-testing the draft by having a fresh, context-free Claude read it as a real reader would.

## Why a skill for this

Long-form collaborative writing benefits from a consistent process — context first, structure second, blind-read testing last — that's easy to skip under ad hoc prompting. Packaging the stages as a skill keeps the discipline even when the user just wants to "write a doc."

## Good for

Drafting PRDs, RFCs, design docs, and proposals that need to hold up when someone unfamiliar with the background reads them cold.
