Solo Skills packages seven bilingual (English/Chinese) skills for solo founders and indie developers — launch tweets, customer emails, decision frameworks, and postmortems.

## Why a skill for this

Solo founders repeat the same small set of communication and decision tasks — announcing a launch, writing a postmortem — often enough that having them as ready skills beats rewriting the format each time.

## Good for

Indie developers and solo founders who want consistent, ready-made formats for the recurring writing and decision tasks running a small product involves.
