These curated Codex skills drive one-command deploys to Vercel, Netlify, Render, or Cloudflare directly from the agent, without a separate manual deploy step.

## Why a skill for this

Each platform has its own CLI and deploy conventions; a skill scoped to a specific target means deploys work correctly on the first try instead of trial-and-error against unfamiliar CLI flags.

## Good for

Shipping a change straight to production or a preview environment as part of the same session that made the change.
