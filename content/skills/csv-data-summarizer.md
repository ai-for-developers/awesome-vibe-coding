The CSV Data Summarizer skill automatically profiles a CSV file and produces insights with visualizations, without needing a detailed prompt describing what to look for.

## Why a skill for this

Most CSV exploration starts with the same handful of questions — distributions, outliers, missing data, obvious correlations. Automating that first pass saves the back-and-forth of asking for it column by column.

## Good for

A fast first look at an unfamiliar CSV dataset before deciding what deeper analysis is worth doing.
