Superpowers Lab is the experimental proving ground for [Superpowers](/skills/superpowers/) — newer, less-settled techniques that graduate into the stable library once they hold up in real use.

## Why a skill for this

Skill development benefits from the same staging discipline as software releases — trying new techniques in a lab environment before committing them to a widely-installed stable marketplace avoids regressions for everyone else.

## Good for

Superpowers users who want early access to newer skills and are comfortable with some rough edges.
