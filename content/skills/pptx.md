The PPTX skill packages the conventions needed to generate and edit PowerPoint presentations: slide layouts, text formatting, speaker notes, and basic chart or image placement, producing files that open cleanly in PowerPoint or Keynote.

## Why a skill for this

Presentation files have their own layout model that doesn't map cleanly onto plain text generation. A dedicated skill means slides come out with sensible structure — titles, bullet hierarchy, consistent formatting — instead of a wall of text dumped onto one slide.

## Good for

Turning a report or outline into a first-draft deck, and making targeted edits to specific slides in an existing presentation.
