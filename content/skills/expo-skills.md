Expo Skills is the official skill set from the Expo team for building React Native apps with Expo, covering the framework's specific conventions and tooling.

## Why a skill for this

Being maintained by the Expo team itself means the skill tracks the framework's actual conventions and release changes, rather than a third party's best guess at Expo's current best practices.

## Good for

React Native projects built on Expo that want framework-accurate guidance straight from the maintainers.
