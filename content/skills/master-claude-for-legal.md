This skill pack equips Claude for legal-team work — NDA triage, multi-party contract version diffing, citation verification, and meeting briefs — with reference docs on privilege and long-document review baked in.

## Why a skill for this

Legal document review has domain-specific pitfalls (privilege, precise citation formats, version tracking across parties) that generic document handling misses. Packaging those conventions as reference material keeps the output usable by an actual legal team.

## Good for

Legal or compliance teams handling contract review, NDA triage, or citation-heavy document work.
