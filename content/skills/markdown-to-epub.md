This skill converts markdown documents and chat summaries into properly formatted EPUB ebook files, handling the packaging EPUB readers expect.

## Why a skill for this

EPUB is a zipped bundle of XHTML, metadata, and a manifest — not something you get right by hand-writing markup. A skill that knows the format's requirements produces a file that actually opens cleanly in e-readers.

## Good for

Turning long-form markdown content or research output into a distributable ebook.
