The Brand Guidelines skill encodes a specific brand's rules — color palette, typography, tone of voice, logo usage — so an agent's output stays consistent with that identity across many separate requests.

## Why a skill for this

Without a persistent reference, brand consistency degrades over a long working session as each new prompt reintroduces slightly different defaults. Packaging the guidelines once means every subsequent output pulls from the same source of truth.

## Good for

Marketing copy, design assets, and any recurring content production where a consistent brand identity matters more than one-off creative variety.
