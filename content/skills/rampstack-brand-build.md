This 59-skill library covers a website's full lifecycle — brand, design, content, SEO, development, ops, and growth — including a dedicated SEO audit suite.

## Why a skill for this

Building and running a marketing site touches many disciplines that don't usually live in one toolkit. Bundling them together keeps brand, content, and technical SEO work consistent instead of drifting apart across separate tools.

## Good for

Small teams or solo builders running a marketing site end-to-end without a specialist for each discipline.
