Qwen3-Coder-Next is built for self-hosted coding agents — an 80B-total, 3B-active mixture-of-experts model under an Apache 2.0 license, small enough to run on a single well-equipped workstation instead of a data-center cluster.

## Why it stands out

- **Apache 2.0 license**, with no restrictions on commercial use, fine-tuning, or redistribution.
- **80B total / 3B active parameters** — the active-parameter count is what determines inference cost and hardware needs, and it's genuinely runnable locally.
- **Cheapest hosted pricing on this list** for teams that don't want to self-host but still want open-weight economics.

## Good for

Developers and teams that want a coding model they fully control — running locally, air-gapped, or fine-tuned on private code, without depending on a hosted API.
