Gemini 3.1 Pro is Google's flagship coding and reasoning model — Gemini 3.5 Pro hasn't shipped yet, so 3.1 Pro remains the top tier of the Gemini line for complex, multi-step work.

## Why it stands out

- **1M-token context window**, one of the largest available, well suited to reasoning across a large codebase or long document set at once.
- **Competitive pricing** relative to other frontier models, especially for requests under the 200K-token tier.
- **Deep integration with Google's tooling** — Vertex AI, AI Studio, and the broader Google Cloud ecosystem.

## Good for

Architectural and cross-file work where the model needs to hold a large amount of code or documentation in context at once, rather than working file-by-file.
