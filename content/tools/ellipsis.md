Ellipsis reviews pull requests automatically, leaving comments on bugs and style issues, and can go a step further by opening follow-up commits that fix the problems it finds rather than just describing them.

## Why it stands out

- **Fix-it commits** — beyond commenting, Ellipsis can push a commit that resolves the issue it flagged.
- **Configurable review rules** let teams define what it should and shouldn't comment on.
- **Codebase Q&A** lets developers ask Ellipsis questions about the repo directly from a PR or Slack.

## Good for

Teams that want an AI reviewer that can also close the loop by fixing the issues it identifies.
