Val Town lets you write a small TypeScript script in the browser and run it instantly as a hosted endpoint, cron job, or webhook — no deploy pipeline required — with an AI assistant that can generate and modify vals from a prompt.

## Why it stands out

- **Instant deploy loop.** Saving a val makes it live immediately, with no build step or infrastructure to configure.
- **AI-generated vals**, letting you describe a small tool or automation and get a runnable script back in seconds.
- **Social and shareable**, with vals forkable and discoverable like a cross between GitHub Gists and a scripting playground.

## Good for

Developers who want to prototype small scripts, webhooks, and automations quickly without setting up a project or deployment pipeline.
