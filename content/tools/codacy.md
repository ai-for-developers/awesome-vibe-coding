Codacy runs static analysis, security scanning, and coverage checks on every commit and pull request, surfacing issues as a quality score and letting teams enforce consistent standards across large codebases.

## Why it stands out

- **Broad language coverage** — supports 40+ languages and aggregates dozens of linters and security tools under one dashboard.
- **Quality gates** — lets teams set pass/fail thresholds on PRs for coverage, duplication, and complexity, not just style.
- **Codacy Guardrails** adds real-time security and quality checks for AI-generated code inside the IDE, not just after a PR is opened.

## Good for

Engineering teams that want a centralized, configurable quality and security gate across a polyglot codebase.
