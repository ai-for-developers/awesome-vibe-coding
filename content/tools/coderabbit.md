CodeRabbit reviews every pull request automatically, posting a summary of the change plus inline comments on specific lines, aimed at being the fastest AI reviewer to set up and the least noisy in what it flags.

## Why it stands out

- **Minimal setup** — connect a repo and it starts reviewing, without heavy configuration.
- **Lower noise** than some competitors, favoring fewer, more relevant comments over exhaustive flagging.
- **Broad platform support** across GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket, and Azure DevOps.

## Good for

Teams that want automatic PR review running with minimal setup and low comment noise.
