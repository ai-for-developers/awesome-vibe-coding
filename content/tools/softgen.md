Softgen generates full-stack web apps — UI, backend logic, and database — from a chat interface, aimed at founders and builders who want to go from idea to working product without hand-writing the initial implementation.

## Why it stands out

- **Full-stack by default**, generating backend and database wiring alongside the frontend rather than just static UI.
- **Iterative chat workflow** for refining features and fixing bugs by describing them in plain language.
- **Fast path to a deployed app**, aimed at getting a working product live rather than just a prototype.

## Good for

Non-technical founders and solo builders who want a working full-stack app from a product idea without writing code by hand.
