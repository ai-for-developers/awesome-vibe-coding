Google acquired Galileo AI in 2025 and folded its UI-generation engine into Google Labs as Stitch — a Gemini-powered tool that turns a short text description or an uploaded wireframe into polished, editable interface designs, complete with exportable front-end code.

## Why it stands out

- **Gemini-powered generation**, carrying over Galileo AI's high-fidelity output while running on Google's latest models and infrastructure.
- **Text-to-UI and wireframe-to-UI**, giving designers a fast starting point from either a prompt or a rough sketch.
- **Free through Google Labs**, with tighter integration into Google's broader AI and design tooling than the original standalone product had.

## Good for

Designers and product teams who want a fast, visually credible first draft of a screen without paying for a dedicated design-generation tool.
