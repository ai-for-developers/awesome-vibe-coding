Blackbox AI is a multi-model coding assistant that works both as a standalone web chat and as an extension across more than 30 IDEs, giving developers code search, autocomplete, image-to-code, and agentic app-building from a single account.

## Why it stands out

- **Broad IDE reach**, with plugins spanning VS Code, JetBrains IDEs, Android Studio, and Xcode rather than one primary editor.
- **Multiple LLMs behind one interface**, so you can switch models without changing tools.
- **App Builder agent** on paid tiers that can scaffold and iterate on a project from a prompt, not just answer inline questions.

## Good for

Developers who want a low-cost entry point into AI coding assistance that works across whichever IDE they already use.
