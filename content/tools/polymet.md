Polymet generates multi-screen product design concepts from a text prompt, giving product designers a set of coherent, on-brand screens to react to and refine instead of starting ideation from a blank file.

## Why it stands out

- **Multi-screen coherence**, generating a set of related screens that share a consistent visual system, not one isolated mockup.
- **Product-design framing**, oriented around flows and screens rather than single marketing pages.
- **Editable output** that designers can restyle and rearrange rather than treat as a finished artifact.

## Good for

Product designers who want a fast, coherent first pass across a multi-screen flow before investing in high-fidelity design.
