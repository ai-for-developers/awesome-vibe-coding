Framer AI generates a complete, responsive website from a short text prompt, dropping the result into Framer's visual canvas where it can be restyled, rearranged, and published without touching code.

## Why it stands out

- **Full-site generation**, producing multiple pages and sections in one pass rather than a single screen.
- **Production-grade hosting and CMS** built into the same platform, so a generated site is publishable immediately.
- **Framer's animation and interaction engine** available on generated output, not just static layout.

## Good for

Marketers, founders, and designers who want a real, publishable website fast without hiring a developer.
