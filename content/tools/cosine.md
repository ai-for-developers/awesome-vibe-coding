Cosine builds an AI software engineer, Genie, that plugs into a team's existing repos and issue tracker to pick up tickets, work through a codebase, and submit pull requests for review rather than just suggesting snippets inline.

## Why it stands out

- **Repo-scale context.** It's built to reason over an entire codebase and its history, not just the files open in an editor.
- **Ticket-to-PR workflow.** Assign it an issue and it works the task through to a reviewable pull request, mirroring how a human engineer would pick up work.
- **Enterprise-first design.** Aimed at engineering teams that want an agent embedded in their existing GitHub/Jira workflow rather than a standalone chat tool.

## Good for

Engineering teams that want to delegate well-scoped backlog tickets to an agent that operates inside their existing PR review process.
