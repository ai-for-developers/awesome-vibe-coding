Lovable is built for people who want to go from an idea to a working product without writing code themselves — you describe the app conversationally, and it scaffolds the frontend, backend, and database, with a visual editor layered on top for tweaks.

## Why it stands out

- **Product-first UX.** Designed for founders and designers, not just engineers.
- **Built-in Supabase integration** for auth, database, and storage without separate setup.
- **Visual editing** on top of the generated code for quick styling changes.

## Good for

Non-technical founders and small teams validating a product idea who want a real, deployable app rather than a static mockup.
