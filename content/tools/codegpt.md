CodeGPT is an IDE extension that adds AI chat, autocomplete, and agent workflows to VS Code and JetBrains IDEs, with a bring-your-own-key model that lets you connect the provider and model of your choice rather than routing through a single fixed backend.

## Why it stands out

- **BYOK by default**, so the free plan works with your own OpenAI, Anthropic, or other provider keys instead of requiring a bundled subscription.
- **Agentic mode with planning**, available as an add-on, for multi-step tasks beyond single-line completions.
- **Multi-IDE support**, with a mature VS Code extension and a JetBrains counterpart for IntelliJ, PyCharm, and WebStorm.

## Good for

Developers who want a flexible, pay-for-what-you-use AI assistant inside their existing IDE without switching editors.
