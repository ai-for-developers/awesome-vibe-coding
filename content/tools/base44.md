Base44 lets non-developers describe a product idea in chat and get back a working, hosted application with a database, authentication, and integrations already wired up, without touching code or assembling separate services.

## Why it stands out

- **All-in-one hosting.** Database, auth, file storage, and deployment are bundled in, so there's no separate backend service to configure.
- **Built for non-developers**, with a conversational editing loop rather than a code view as the primary interface.
- **Fast idea-to-live-app path**, aimed at solo founders shipping internal tools and small products quickly.

## Good for

Non-technical founders and small teams who want a working, hosted app from a plain description without managing infrastructure themselves.
