Visily specializes in getting a rough idea into an editable wireframe fast — importing screenshots of existing apps, sketches, or prompts and turning them into a design that non-designers can still rearrange and present.

## Why it stands out

- **Screenshot import**, capturing an existing app's UI into editable, on-canvas components as a starting point.
- **No-design-skill workflow**, built for PMs and founders rather than assuming Figma fluency.
- **Large pattern library** of pre-built UI blocks that speed up assembling a believable mockup.

## Good for

Non-designers — PMs, founders, researchers — who need a credible, editable mockup without learning a full design tool.
