Tabnine focuses on enterprise privacy and control: it can run fully air-gapped or in a private cloud, and its Enterprise Context Engine tunes suggestions to an organization's own codebase rather than relying solely on public training data.

## Why it stands out

- **Deployment flexibility**, including fully on-premises or VPC-isolated setups for regulated industries.
- **No public free tier** — it's built and priced specifically for enterprise procurement, not individual signup.
- **Recognized as a Gartner Magic Quadrant Visionary** for enterprise AI coding agents two years running.

## Good for

Regulated organizations that need AI coding assistance without code or context leaving their own infrastructure.
