Databutton pairs a chat-driven agent with a workspace for building internal tools, dashboards, and small full-stack apps, generating Python backends and React frontends and handling hosting so teams can ship without setting up infrastructure.

## Why it stands out

- **Aimed at internal tools**, a niche many general-purpose app builders don't specifically target — dashboards, ops tools, and admin panels.
- **Python-based generated backends**, useful for teams that want generated code closer to a stack they can maintain themselves.
- **Integrated hosting and secrets management**, so apps go from generated to deployed without extra setup.

## Good for

Teams that want to spin up internal tools and small business apps quickly without dedicating engineering time to them.
