Uizard generates editable UI mockups from text prompts, hand-drawn sketches, or screenshots, letting non-designers produce a workable prototype and hand developers something closer to spec than a napkin drawing.

## Why it stands out

- **Sketch-to-UI conversion**, turning a photographed whiteboard wireframe into an editable digital layout.
- **Text-prompt design generation** for teams that want to start from a description rather than a blank canvas.
- **Theme and rebranding tools** that restyle an entire mockup's color and typography in one pass.

## Good for

Founders, PMs, and non-designers who need a credible UI prototype fast, without starting a Figma file from scratch.
