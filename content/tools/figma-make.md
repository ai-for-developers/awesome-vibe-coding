Figma Make builds on Figma's design surface directly, letting teams turn a prompt or an existing frame into an interactive prototype, and iterate on it with follow-up prompts without leaving Figma.

## Why it stands out

- **Native Figma integration**, working from the same files and design system teams already maintain, with no export step.
- **Prompt-driven iteration**, refining a generated prototype through conversation rather than manual rebuilds.
- **Interactive, working output**, going beyond static mockups to prototypes with real logic and state.

## Good for

Teams already living in Figma who want to move from design to an interactive prototype without switching tools.
