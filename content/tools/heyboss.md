HeyBoss positions itself as an AI "product team in a chat window" — describe a website, app, or landing page and it generates, hosts, and lets you keep iterating on it conversationally, aimed squarely at non-developers.

## Why it stands out

- **Conversational-only workflow**, with no code editor exposed — every change is made by chatting with the assistant.
- **Covers web and simple mobile app output** from the same chat interface, rather than being limited to one surface.
- **Built-in hosting**, so a generated app is live and shareable without a separate deployment step.

## Good for

Non-technical founders who want to go from an idea to a live, hosted app entirely through conversation.
