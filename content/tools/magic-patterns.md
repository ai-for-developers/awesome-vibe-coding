Magic Patterns turns a text prompt or rough sketch into a working React UI, aimed at product managers and designers who want to validate an idea's interface before it becomes an engineering ticket.

## Why it stands out

- **Prototype-first framing**, optimized for fast exploration of UI directions rather than final production code.
- **Design-system awareness**, letting teams point it at existing components so generated screens match the product's real look.
- **Editable, real code output**, so a promising prototype can be handed to engineers as a starting point rather than thrown away.

## Good for

Product teams who want to explore and validate UI ideas in realistic, clickable form before scoping engineering work.
